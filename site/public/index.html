STRATUM

Dig down, build up, and survive the night. A world of blocks to explore, alone or with friends.

A whole world under your feet

Every hill, cave and village is made of blocks you can break and place. Gather wood, make tools, dig for ore, and build anything you can imagine.

Villages and trading

Find villages out in the plains and forests. Farmers, smiths, librarians, shepherds and butchers each trade their own goods for jade.

Explore with compass and map

A compass that points home, a clock for the hour, and maps that fill in as you walk.

Boats and minecarts

Row across the sea, or lay rails that curve, climb hills and speed you along with boosters.

Spark power

Wire levers, buttons and pressure plates to lamps, doors and repeaters. Build traps, gates and secret doors.

Days, nights and weather

Rain, storms and snow roll in. When night falls, creatures come out. Build a bed and a shelter before dark.

The Ember Deep

Build an obsidian frame, light it with a fire striker, and step through into a burning underworld of lava seas and glowing ore.

The Cinder Colossus

Brick keeps stand on stilts above the lava. Something huge waits inside each one. Bring your best armour.

Sky Islands

Start on a floating island above a sea of clouds. Bridge your way across snowy, sandy and wooded islands. Just don't look down.

Themed worlds

Make a world your way: Frozen Wilds, Endless Dunes, the Archipelago, Giant Peaks, or Flatlands for building.

World types

Towering Wilds stretches everything skyward with cliffs, overhangs and arches. Vast Lands makes every region four times as wide. Or pick one biome for the whole world.

Build a home

Plaster and timber-frame walls, clay roof tiles, whitewashed and dark planks, polished stone, pillars and lattice windows.

And furnish it

Bookshelves, tables and stools, carpets, potted plants, lanterns, a hearth to light the room, and barrels to keep things in.

Sixteen colours and a houseful of furniture

Wool, carpet, plaster and floor tiles in sixteen colours, painted planks, more stairs, slabs, walls, fences and doors, and furniture from sofas and stoves to pianos, paintings and chandeliers.

Castle Keep

A ready-built castle with a moat, towers, a great hall, a throne room and a dungeon.

Market Town

Furnished houses, an inn, a bakery, a library and a market square. Move in, or build it bigger.

Sky Run

Six stages of parkour over water, each harder than the last, up to a golden tower. Fall in and swim back.

Hedge Maze

A big maze with a prize in the middle. Climb the lookout first, then find your way.

Change the look

Classic's crisp pixels are the usual look; Stratum, Chunky, Storybook, Pastel or Inked repaint every block in another style with one button. Everything on the Extras page is free.

Make your own outfit

Pick a hat, a cape and the colour of every piece. Your friends see it when you play together. Deer, foxes, rabbits, penguins, goats and camels now roam the world too.

The Quiet and the Hollow King

Make a gate stone and touch it. It carries you to pale islands hanging in the dark, where the Hollow King waits at his throne. Take his crown if you can.

New things to meet

Globs that bound about and break in two, Bloats that creep up and burst, Shades that step through the air, and Snappers in deep water.

A hound for a friend

A few bones and a wild hound is yours: it follows you, sits when told and sees off monsters. Feed a camel and you can ride it.

Colour, carts and a guard

Sixteen dyes for wool and for sheep, carts that roll by themselves, a chest cart, and a guard in every village.

Machines that do the work

Pistons, a dispenser, an observer, a comparator, a detector rail, a daylight sensor, a note block, and chutes that pick things up and pass them on.

Better with friends

Stratum has online multiplayer built in. Make an account in the game, join a server, and build together.

  • Your account keeps your things, wherever you log in.
  • Talk with push-to-talk voice chat. Headsets work too.
  • Pick a world: survival, creative, Sky Islands, Towering Wilds, Vast Lands and more.
  • Free Extras: nine ready-made worlds and nine outfits, one click each.
  • Type to chat, and share the same world, day and weather.

Whoever runs your server will give you its address and, if there is one, an invite code. In the game, choose Multiplayer and enter them. For our own server the address is already filled in, and typing play always reaches it.

How to install

It takes about a minute. Your worlds are kept when you install a newer version.

Windows 10 or 11

  1. Download Stratum-0.16.6-Setup.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. (Small games like this one aren't signed by Microsoft.)
  3. Click Install. You don't need an administrator password.
  4. Play from the Stratum icon on your desktop or in the Start menu.
Download the installer

Prefer no installer? Get the zip, unzip it, and run Stratum.exe. To remove Stratum, use Settings › Apps.

Linux

  1. Open a terminal and paste this: 
    curl -fsSL https://stratum.rushingtechnologies.com/install.sh | sh
  2. Start Stratum from your applications menu, or type stratum.

Or download the tarball, extract it and run ./install.sh (or just ./Stratum.x86_64). To remove it: sh ~/.local/share/stratum/install.sh --uninstall.
